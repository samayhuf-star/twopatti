# App Review notes — 2 Patti 1.0.0 (build 3)

Paste this into App Review Information. Bundle ID com.samayvashsht.twopatti. ASC app id 6833949427.

2 Patti is an offline two-card card game inspired by Teen Patti. It is entertainment only. There is no login and no account. It is not a cash Teen Patti room: each player holds two cards, Centre mode shares one table card, and Imaginary mode completes each hand with one picked third card.

## How to play a hand

1. Install this build and open it. Nothing needs a password, a code, or a network connection.
2. On first launch the app asks if you are 17 or older. Tap “I AM 17 OR OLDER”. The table does not open until you do. “I AM UNDER 17” stops play and asks you to close the app. On iPhone, BACK returns to that question.
3. The lobby starts you with 25,000 virtual chips. Pick Centre or Imaginary, then 4 or 5 seats. Tap SIT on Silver (ante 100), Gold (ante 500), or Diamond (ante 5,000, minimum 25,000). All three can be sat with the starting balance.
4. Centre: one shared card is dealt face down and opens after the first betting round. It becomes every remaining player’s third card, then a second betting round starts.
5. Imaginary: there is no shared card. The game picks one third card for your two cards. Both of your real cards stay in your hand. The info button next to the Imaginary description explains this.
6. On your turn you can stay blind and bet the cheaper stake, or tap SEE (free) to look at your cards. After you see, bets cost more and you stay seen. FOLD drops you out of the hand. When two players are left, SHOW pays to compare the hands.
7. Every opponent is a computer player and is labelled BOT. They act on their own. You do not need another person or another device.
8. The best three-card hand wins the pot in virtual chips. A-2-3 is the highest straight. If the hands are equal, suits of each player’s own two cards decide the winner, so there is always one winner. A player who wins while still blind also receives a house bonus of 10 percent of the pot. That bonus is not taken from the other players.
9. The book icon opens How to play. The school icon opens a short three-step explanation of Centre, Imaginary, and blind versus seen. The gear icon opens Settings.

## Business model

Version 1.0 is free. There is no in-app purchase, no subscription, no trial, and no chip shop. Virtual chips cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or cashed out. They have no cash value and there are no prizes. In-app purchase is not in this binary.

## Gambling and age

This is simulated gambling. There is no real-money wagering and no cash-out. The age rating is 17+, and the in-app notice uses the same age. The age question is a declaration, not an identity check.

## Data and services

Chips, the age confirmation, and the vibration setting are stored only on the device. Deleting the app removes them. The game does not contact a server. The only network use is when a player chooses to open Privacy, Terms, or Support. There is no advertising and no analytics SDK. Support email: samayhuf@gmail.com.
