**Description**
2 Patti is a two-card social card game inspired by Teen Patti. Everyone gets two cards. The third card comes from the table mode you pick, and the best three-card hand wins the pot.

This is simulated gambling for entertainment. Chips are virtual. They have no cash value, they cannot be purchased, and they cannot be cashed out. There is no real-money wagering and no prizes.

FEATURES
- Centre mode: one shared card opens after the first betting round
- Imaginary mode: your best third card is picked for you
- Play offline against computer opponents labelled BOT
- Blind and seen betting, fold, and show
- A local chip wallet and a daily bonus of virtual chips
- A 17+ age gate before the first hand

No account is required. Chips and settings stay on this device.
